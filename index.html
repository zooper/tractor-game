Tips for new players

Shifts: snow keeps falling from 22:00 to 06:00 and you're paid for keeping the main streets open. Free plow: no new snow, no clock. Performance mode uses a coarser snow grid, fewer trees and lights; Auto turns it on for phones and tablets. Both apply when a map starts.

Garage

Earn money with the gravel pit's day takings and the snow shifts' pay. Upgrades fit every machine on every map, from the next time a map starts.
